Welcome to North Star Bakery

Here at North Star Bakery, every loaf starts before sunrise. We use simple ingredients, local grains, and time, because good bread cannot be rushed. Stop by and taste the difference, or place a pre-order and we will have something fresh waiting for you.

What We're Known For

Our neighborhood bakery specializes in slow-fermented artisan breads, handmade pastries, and celebration cakes made in small batches. We focus on dependable quality, warm service, and baked goods that feel at home on a family table, at the office, or at a special event.

Weekend Pastry Box

A rotating assortment of fresh cookies and pastries selected by our bakers. It is a convenient choice for families, office gatherings, and weekend guests. Contact us to reserve a box for pickup.
