Our Story

North Star Bakery was created as a neighborhood bakery where customers can count on fresh bread, friendly service, and handmade food. Our bakers begin before sunrise so commuters can pick up something fresh in the morning and families can bring quality bread home at the end of the day.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe simple ingredients deserve careful attention. Whenever practical, we work with local and regional grain suppliers and choose ingredients for freshness, flavor, and consistent quality. We also use seasonal ingredients in selected pastries and cakes so our menu can reflect the community and time of year.

Meet the Team
